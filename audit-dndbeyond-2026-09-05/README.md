# Universal art audit — review package

Start with **CLAUDE-REVIEW.md**, then use **REVIEW-GALLERY.html** to inspect flagged originals. **FABLE-NOTE.md** covers edition mapping and scope. **missing-and-mapping-candidates.csv** lists proposals; mapping-only proposals do not automatically require new art.

**full-sweep-ledger.csv** (also JSON) is the complete 1,408-row audit record. **reviewed-notes.json** contains the final observations. **source-comparisons.json** identifies direct official-source comparisons.

The earlier **provisional-notes.json** and **other-provisional-notes.json** are working notes, superseded by **reviewed-notes.json** and the final ledger. In particular, the initial Sahuagin Baron limb-count concern was withdrawn after inspecting the original: four arms and two legs are present.

Review recommendations are not retirement decisions. All active images were visually screened, but only the specifically marked subset received a direct official artwork or text comparison. D&D Beyond access here was public, without the user's signed-in entitlements.
