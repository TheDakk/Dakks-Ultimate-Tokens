# Changelog

## 4.5.0 — The setting creatures and the bestiaries (2026-11-12)

There was no separate 4.4: its setting creatures ship here, rolled into 4.5. 533 creatures from thirteen setting books and adventures, and 2,999 creatures from eleven third-party 5e bestiaries, every one painted, 950 of them with a female or male twin; every third-party text is written in our own words.

**Added**

- 533 creatures from the setting books and adventures (Curse of Strahd, Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica, Eberron: Rising from the Last War, Explorer's Guide to Wildemount, Strixhaven, Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft, Planescape, Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen, Dragon of Icespire Peak, Spelljammer, Bigby Presents: Glory of the Giants, Call of the Netherdeep, Minsc and Boo's Journal of Villainy), 136 of them with a female or male twin
- 2,999 creatures from third-party 5e bestiaries (Tome of Beasts 2 and 3, Monster Manual Expanded I to III, Grim Hollow: The Monster Grimoire, The Infernal Tome, Ultimate Bestiary: Revenge of the Horde, Big Bad Creatures, Big Book of Big Bads, Fifth Edition Foes), 814 of them with a female or male twin, their mechanics converted and every text written in our own words
- The new creatures in Creatures and Creatures by Challenge, foldered by type and challenge like the rest, each naming its book

**Changed**

- The download is larger, about 700 MB (about 800 MB installed): 4,390 new paintings
- The vampire spawn's twin is now a man, since its painting is a woman; the skulk and the ghost knight lose the female twins 4.3.0 gave them by mistake

**Fixed**

- 65 creatures in 4.3.0 pointed at paintings that release did not carry and showed no portrait or token: the SRD kobold, death dog, blood hawk, vampire spawn and adult chromatic dragons, and creatures from Monsters of the Multiverse, Tomb of Annihilation and other books that share a name with a later bestiary's creature. Each has its own painting again
- 177 creatures from earlier releases had stat lines or actions a scan had misread, now read off the printed page: flying and swimming speeds that were missing (the atropal, the brain in a jar, the vampiric ixitxachitl), senses, skills, saving throws and alignments, and the traits of the Storm King's Thunder townsfolk, which sat in their Languages line
- Some creature texts from the 4.3 release no longer point to a book's appendices, which the module does not contain: the Elemental Evil cultists' spell notes, Acererak, Rezmir, Sir Braford and the Zuggtmoy spores

## 4.3.0 — The 2014 core options, the Artificer and the Forgotten Realms adventures (2026-11-08)

What the SRD leaves out of the 2014 Player's Handbook and Dungeon Master's Guide, the Artificer, 260 creatures from nine Forgotten Realms adventures, and more than 400 further character options, spells and magic items, each with its own painting; Theros now sits in the main 5e compendia. There was no separate 4.2: what was planned for it (the 2014 core options and the Artificer) ships in this release.

**Added**

- The 2014 Player's Handbook beyond the SRD: its races and subraces, subclasses, backgrounds, feats and spells, and the Dungeon Master's Guide's magic items, each with its own painting
- The Artificer class from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, with its class features
- 260 creatures from the Forgotten Realms adventures (Tomb of Annihilation, Out of the Abyss, Princes of the Apocalypse, Tyranny of Dragons, Descent into Avernus, Tales from the Yawning Portal, Storm King's Thunder, Rime of the Frostmaiden, Lost Mine of Phandelver), 56 of them with a female or male twin
- Magic items and spells from those adventures, and magic items, spells and character options from further sourcebooks (among them the Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, Acquisitions Incorporated, Ravnica, Eberron, Wildemount, Strixhaven, Van Richten's Guide, Dragonlance, Spelljammer, Glory of the Giants and the Plane Shift supplements): more than 400 documents, each with its own painting
- Ready characters of each sex for every new race, subclass and background in the 5e Characters compendium

**Changed**

- Mythic Odysseys of Theros is part of the main 5e compendia: its creatures, races, subclasses, backgrounds and items sit beside the other sourcebooks' and name their book. The separate Theros compendia, the Art Library (Theros) catalogue and the Theros Characters compendium are gone; the Theros paintings are catalogued in Art Library (All)
- Where a later book revised an adventure creature (Monsters of the Multiverse), only the revision is kept; where two books print the same race, the better painting is used
- The download is about 400 MB

**Fixed**

- 890 paintings re-keyed from their original captures with the improved keyer: no raspberry fringe on glows, spirits and magic circles
- Bows and crossbows across the library redrawn or retouched so that every string runs straight from tip to tip, or from each tip to the nock on a drawn bow and to the latch on a crossbow

**Requirements:** Foundry VTT 13 or later for the art, Art Library (All) and the 5e compendia (verified on 14.369). The 5e compendia need the D&D 5e system 5.3.3 or later, including 6.x (tested on 6.0.6, which needs Foundry VTT 14). The 2e compendia need Foundry VTT 14 and the Advanced Roleplaying System 2026.04.27 or later (tested on 2026.10.06), with its ruleset variant set to 2. The art and Art Library (All) work in any system.

## 4.1.0 — Ready characters, Proficient Use and token rings (2026-10-09)

Ready-made characters of each sex for every painted race, class, subclass and background, the Dark Sun Proficient Use rules, optional dynamic token rings, corrected races, and every document with its own painting.

**Added**

- Characters compendia (5e, Theros, 2e and Dark Sun): a ready portrait actor of each sex for every painted race, class, subclass and background, 411 in all, its painting already its portrait and token; one each for the thri-kreen, tohr-kreen and Marnitan lizardfolk, painted once. The GM imports one and gives a player ownership
- Proficient Use for Dark Sun: each Dark Sun weapon whose source gives one states its Proficient Use rule, and a new Documentation (Dark Sun) compendium holds the rules for the seven core weapons used on Athas
- Dynamic token rings for Dakk's creatures: a world setting, off by default, that gives tokens placed from this module's creatures Foundry's dynamic token ring around their painting; in 2e worlds the ring flashes red on damage and green on healing
- Start here in each Art Library now explains how to open the compendium folders, place creatures, use the Characters compendia and choose a portrait by hand
- New paintings for the Astral Projection power, the Whispers spell and the Dark Sun weapons reference: every document in the module now has its own painting (6,176 in all)

**Changed**

- The hydra, dracohydra and necrohydra are repainted facing the viewer, every neck rising from the chest, and the Dark Sun water elementals as living water
- The Theros Centaur, Minotaur and Triton name Monsters of the Multiverse as their book, and the Theros Human the SRD, as the text they carry comes from there
- Requirements: the 2e compendia need Foundry VTT 14 and the Advanced Roleplaying System 2026.04.27 or later
- Tested on the D&D 5e system 6.0.6 and the Advanced Roleplaying System 2026.10.06; the 5e compendia still need dnd5e 5.3.3 or later
- Links that pointed into the D&D 5e system's own monster compendium now point at this module's copy of the same creature, so they resolve on dnd5e 5.x and 6.x alike

**Fixed**

- Sourcebook and Theros races were built on the Dragonborn: their creature type, darkvision and flying speeds are now their own (44 races)
- Flying sourcebook and Theros creatures that hover now carry hover
- The 5e Sling Bullet showed the 2e painting
- A misspelt icon path on an SRD spell, and build bookkeeping fields that should not have shipped

**Requirements:** Foundry VTT 13 or later for the art, Art Library (All) and the 5e compendia (verified on 14.368). The 5e compendia need the D&D 5e system 5.3.3 or later, including 6.x (tested on 6.0.6, which needs Foundry VTT 14). The 2e compendia need Foundry VTT 14 and the Advanced Roleplaying System 2026.04.27 or later (tested on 2026.10.06), with its ruleset variant set to 2. The art and Art Library (All) work in any system.

## 4.0.0 — Everything in one module (2026-10-08)

Dakk's Ultimate Tokens: painted art for Dungeons & Dragons in Foundry VTT and the game compendia that use it, D&D 5e (2014) and AD&D 2e in one module, each compendium shown only in worlds of its own system.

**Added**

- D&D 5e (2014) compendia: the SRD 5.1 monsters, spells, items, classes and character options, joined kind by kind with creatures, spells, magic items, subclasses, races, backgrounds and feats from the Monster Manual, Monsters of the Multiverse, Fizban's, Xanathar's, Tasha's, Fiendish Folio, Tome of Beasts, Flee, Mortals! and Dr Dhrolin's: 2,052 creatures, 443 spells and 1,037 items
- Every sourcebook's rules text and creature descriptions written in our own words, with the game numbers unchanged
- Theros for 5e: 70 creatures, magic items, races, subclasses and a background, with the 101 paintings of the Theros collection
- AD&D 2e compendia for the Advanced Roleplaying System, from For Gold & Glory: 165 creatures, 484 wizard and priest spells, classes, races, skills, proficiencies, equipment, weapons, armour and tables
- Dark Sun for 2e: 219 creatures, 22 races, classes, psionic powers and abilities, Athasian weapons, armour and shields, alchemy and poisons, fighting styles, and 271 priest and wizard spells; and its first campaign, Freedom: NPCs, items and treasure, handouts and tables (statistics only, no adventure text)
- Creatures by Challenge: a twin of every creature compendium sorted by challenge rating (2e by hit dice)
- 6,173 paintings in one classic oil-painting hand: the generic library of 4,899 for any system, the Dark Sun collection (1,173) and Theros (101); each a transparent WebP sized to its token, with a woman beside the man for every people
- Dark Sun paintings for every Athasian document: Athasian weapons, mounts and classes, the peoples of Athas and their women, armour and shields, alchemy, and an emblem for each psionic power, fighting style and spell (two, the Astral Projection power and the Whispers spell, carry placeholder icons for now)
- Art Library catalogues by setting: Art Library (All) in every world, Art Library (Dark Sun) in 2e worlds and Art Library (Theros) in 5e worlds, every kind browsed by its own groups (spells by level, weapons by family and so on)
- Compendiums sit under Dakk's Ultimate Tokens > D&D 5e (2014) or D&D 2e in the sidebar, in matching kind folders (Creatures, Spells, Items, a classes folder and Reference, with Skills & Proficiencies in 2e); inside each, creatures are foldered by type (and by challenge rating in their own Creatures by Challenge pack, hit dice in 2e), spells by level (2e by wizard and priest list, then level), 5e races by family and items by kind
- Packs open in their intended order (challenge ratings and hit dice numerically, Cantrip before 1st Level, each Art Library from Start here); a pack whose order you have set with its sort button keeps your choice
- Every actor carries its painting as portrait and token, with a female or male twin beside it where one is painted

**Requirements:** Foundry VTT 13 or later, verified on 14.368. The 5e compendia need the D&D 5e system 5.x (tested on 5.3.3); the 2e compendia need the Advanced Roleplaying System (tested on 2026.08.25) with its ruleset variant set to 2. The art and Art Library (All) work in any system.
