# Changelog

## 4.0.0 — Everything in one module (2026-10-08)

Dakk's Ultimate Tokens: painted art for Dungeons & Dragons in Foundry VTT and the game compendia that use it, D&D 5e (2014) and AD&D 2e in one module, each compendium shown only in worlds of its own system.

**Added**

- D&D 5e (2014) compendia: the SRD 5.1 monsters, spells, items, classes and character options, joined kind by kind with creatures, spells, magic items, subclasses, races, backgrounds and feats from the Monster Manual, Monsters of the Multiverse, Fizban's, Xanathar's, Tasha's, Fiendish Folio, Tome of Beasts, Flee, Mortals! and Dr Dhrolin's: 2,052 creatures, 443 spells and 1,037 items
- Every sourcebook's rules text and creature descriptions written in our own words, with the game numbers unchanged
- Theros for 5e: 70 creatures, magic items, races, subclasses and a background, with the 101 paintings of the Theros collection
- AD&D 2e compendia for the Advanced Roleplaying System, from For Gold & Glory: 165 creatures, 484 wizard and priest spells, classes, races, skills, proficiencies, equipment, weapons, armour and tables
- Dark Sun for 2e: 219 creatures, 22 races, classes, psionic powers and abilities, Athasian weapons, armour and shields, alchemy and poisons, fighting styles, and 271 priest and wizard spells; and its first campaign, Freedom: NPCs, items and treasure, handouts and tables (statistics only, no adventure text)
- Creatures by Challenge: a twin of every creature compendium sorted by challenge rating (2e by hit dice)
- 6,173 paintings in one classic oil-painting hand: the generic library of 4,899 for any system, the Dark Sun collection (1,173) and Theros (101); each a transparent WebP sized to its token, with a woman beside the man for every people
- Dark Sun paintings for every Athasian document: Athasian weapons, mounts and classes, the peoples of Athas and their women, armour and shields, alchemy, and an emblem for each psionic power, fighting style and spell (two, the Astral Projection power and the Whispers spell, carry placeholder icons for now)
- Art Library catalogues by setting: Art Library (All) in every world, Art Library (Dark Sun) in 2e worlds and Art Library (Theros) in 5e worlds, every kind browsed by its own groups (spells by level, weapons by family and so on)
- Compendiums sit under Dakk's Ultimate Tokens > D&D 5e (2014) or D&D 2e in the sidebar, in matching kind folders (Creatures, Spells, Items, a classes folder and Reference, with Skills & Proficiencies in 2e); inside each, creatures are foldered by type (and by challenge rating in their own Creatures by Challenge pack, hit dice in 2e), spells by level (2e by wizard and priest list, then level), 5e races by family and items by kind
- Packs open in their intended order (challenge ratings and hit dice numerically, Cantrip before 1st Level, each Art Library from Start here); a pack whose order you have set with its sort button keeps your choice
- Every actor carries its painting as portrait and token, with a female or male twin beside it where one is painted

**Requirements:** Foundry VTT 13 or later, verified on 14.368. The 5e compendia need the D&D 5e system 5.x (tested on 5.3.3); the 2e compendia need the Advanced Roleplaying System (tested on 2026.08.25) with its ruleset variant set to 2. The art and Art Library (All) work in any system.
